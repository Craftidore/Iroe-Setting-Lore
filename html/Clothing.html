Clothing

While magic users make mass production slightly more feasible, in practice this is still a medieval society, and the disparity of possessions between economic classes is pretty large. Consequently, most commoners, and even a fair few adventurers, use ropes for belts, and clothing which is primarily clipped together instead of sewn. Few peasant farmers or hunter would prioritize purchasing a belt, made of treated leather with a forged metal clasp, over food, tools, or other things which would directly help them provide for their families.

This rule of thumb applies less to races who live more than a few centuries. The "boots" theory of economics applies even more to the peoples of such races. Elves and Dwarves place especially high value on gear which lasts for a long time, and even those of the lower-class try to save up for higher quality clothing, tools, and .

Another major exception to this are soldiers. Even low-paid soldiers and militamen are usually given well-made clothing, as any clothing which might fall off or get in the way in a battle would be a major risk.
