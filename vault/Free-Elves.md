# Free Elves

The 'Free Elves' are more… lax than their [[Wood-Elf]] counterparts.
They, recognizing that the new world they found was *not* the Sky Biome, believe Elves should integrate with the other races, even to the point of intermarrying.
For all their clarity of thought, they have trouble sticking with commitments:
despite their long-lives, they have serious fear of lost opportunities.

## Genetics

Free elves, practically recognizing the limitations of their genetic inability to reproduce, carefully changed their own culture to encourage marrying non-elf species, especially normal humans.
